# Email new paid social candidate leads to recruiters

Each new candidate who answers your Facebook lead form is added to a tracking sheet and your recruiters get an email with their details and a link to the sheet.

**Apps:** Facebook Lead Ads, Google Sheets, Microsoft Outlook

**Based on:** [Email new paid social candidate leads to recruiters](https://zapier.com/automations/human-resources-hr/recruiting/candidate-screening/email-new-paid-social-candidate-leads-to-recruiters) on zapier.com

## Install

This folder is a Zap template. Paste this into your AI agent:

> Build me this Zap, read how here: https://github.com/zapier/awesome-zaps/tree/main/zaps/email-new-paid-social-candidate-leads-to-recruiters

The agent follows `SKILL.md`: it asks you for the recruiting sheet and the recruiter email addresses, then builds, tests and turns on the Zap in your account.

## Files

- `SKILL.md`: what the agent asks, builds and tests
- `workflow.ts`: the Zap's code
- `config.ts`: its settings; the `REPLACE_…` values are filled in for each person
- `connections/`: the accounts it uses (`facebook-lead-ads-beta`, `google-sheets`, `microsoft-outlook`)
- `triggers/`: what starts it
